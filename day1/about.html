About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture short notes and keep useful information close at hand. Use it to organize reminders, ideas, and details you want to find again.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and enter a title for your note.
  2. Write your note in the text area.
  3. Select Save note to submit it.

Features

  • A simple form for writing notes
  • A clear space for viewing saved notes
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common editing tasks
